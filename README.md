---

## 🌟 Overview

**DustWatch** is an intelligent operations and financial decision engine built natively on the **Odoo 19 backend** for commercial and residential solar installations in arid desert climates:
- **Prices Soiling Deficits:** Quantifies daily electricity output loss due to dust accumulation and converts physical kilowatt-hour deficits into local currency (AED).
- **Algorithmic Breakeven Trigger:** Automatically dispatches a cleaning work order the exact day cumulative forfeited electricity revenue reaches the cost of a single cleaning intervention.
- **Recovery & ROI Audit:** Evaluates post-clean generation recovery to prove client payback (e.g. *"This AED 1,500 clean paid for itself in 23 days"*) and distinguish equipment defects from dust.
- **Water Conservation:** Eliminates ~43 redundant washes per roof annually, saving tens of thousands of liters of scarce desalinated UAE water.

## 🚀 Quickstart: Running DustWatch in Odoo 19

### 1. Add DustWatch to your Odoo Configuration
In `~/odoo-server/odoo.conf`, append the path to this repository:
```ini
addons_path = /Users/mathishasamarawickrama/odoo-server/addons,/Users/mathishasamarawickrama/odoo-server/workshop/Workshop,/Users/mathishasamarawickrama/Desktop/Oodoo hackathon
```

### 2. Start Odoo and Update Module List
```bash
source ~/odoo-server/venv/bin/activate
python ~/odoo-server/odoo-bin -c ~/odoo-server/odoo.conf -d admin -i dustwatch
```

### 3. Open in Browser
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:8069`.
2. Open the **Apps** menu, activate Developer Mode, and verify **DustWatch** is installed.
3. Access **DustWatch** from the top app launcher.

## 🧪 Live Demo Walkthrough (The Dubai Hotel Rooftop Story)

1. **Portfolio Overview:** Open **DustWatch &rarr; Operations &rarr; Solar Sites**. Notice the three pre-loaded installations. Point out **Grand Hyatt Hotel Main Roof** in **Red Alert** (AED 1,515 running loss vs AED 1,500 cleaning cost).
2. **Telemetry Sag:** Click into the Grand Hyatt record and view the **Daily Telemetry** tab or open **Telemetry & Trends**. Observe how generation gradually sags over 35 days, then plunges sharply on Day 35 due to a simulated Shamal Sandstorm.
3. **Cleaning Dispatch Board:** Open **Cleaning Jobs Board**. Notice the cleaning job automatically generated by DustWatch.
4. **Execution & Audit:** Move the job to **Cleaned & Verified**. Notice how the site's running loss resets to AED 0, and the system records the post-clean recovery (~AED 66/day) with a calculated **23-day payback**.
